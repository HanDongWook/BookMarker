# Privacy Policy for BookMarker

Effective date: 2026-03-02

BookMarker ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the BookMarker Android app.

## 1) Summary

- BookMarker does not require account sign-up or login.
- Your imported bookmark data is intended to be stored locally on your device.
- We use Firebase services for analytics, crash reporting, and performance monitoring in release builds.

## 2) Information We Process

### A. Information you provide in the app

- Imported bookmark files and bookmark metadata (such as title and URL) are processed to provide app features.
- This data is stored locally on your device for app functionality.

### B. Automatically collected technical data

In release builds, the app uses Google Firebase SDKs:

- Firebase Analytics
- Firebase Crashlytics
- Firebase Performance Monitoring

These services may collect technical information such as:

- app instance identifiers
- device and OS information
- app usage events
- crash logs and diagnostics
- performance traces

This information helps us improve app stability and quality.

## 3) Network Use and External Requests

The app may connect to the network to:

- open external links you choose to open (for example, bookmark links or help pages),
- load website favicons (including fallback favicon requests based on bookmark domains),
- send analytics/crash/performance data through Firebase SDKs.

## 4) Data Storage and Backup

- App data is stored on-device.
- Depending on your Android backup settings and device environment, app data may be included in system backup/restore mechanisms.

## 5) Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell personal data.

We use third-party services that may process technical data:

- Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring)

Please review Google's privacy documentation for more details:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6) Children's Privacy

BookMarker is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law in your region.

## 7) Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

## 8) Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at the same URL with a revised "Effective date."
